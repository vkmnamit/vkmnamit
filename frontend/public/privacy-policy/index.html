Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Kautix processes personal and school information while providing its school management platform.

Information we process

We may process account, contact, academic, attendance, fee, payment, communication, and technical usage information needed to operate the platform.

Why we process information

Information is used to provide requested services, authenticate users, apply role-based access, maintain security, support school operations, and improve reliability.

School control of data

Schools decide which student, parent, teacher, and operational data is entered. Kautix processes that information to provide the service under the school’s instructions.

Access, sharing, and security

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with authorized users, service providers required to operate Kautix, or where required by applicable law. We use authenticated access, role-based permissions, encrypted transport, and activity logging to protect information.

Retention and your choices

Information is retained only for as long as needed to provide the service or meet applicable obligations. Contact your school administrator to request access, correction, or deletion of school-managed information.
